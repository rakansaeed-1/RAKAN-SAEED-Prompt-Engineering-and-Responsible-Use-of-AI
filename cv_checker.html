CV Draft Checker

Paste the output of the CV Tailor assistant. The checker reads section 2 and tests whether every line carries a valid fact ID, whether a certificate, tool, number or city slipped in, and whether the length and format are right. Then it gives you a clean copy without the fact IDs, ready to paste into your CV.
